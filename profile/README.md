Praxis lets businesses bring AI agents into their websites, apps and operations, and serve their own customers by their own rules. Customer relationships, data and brands stay with the business.

Praxis 让企业和服务提供方把智能体接进网站、App 和业务系统，按自己的规则服务客户；客户关系、数据和品牌归你。

## Products · 产品

### [Praxis Work](https://work.deepraxis.com)

Say one sentence, and an agent works on a cloud computer for you: it sorts files, builds spreadsheets, writes weekly reports, makes slides and looks things up, then hands you the files.

说一句话，智能体在云端电脑上替你整理文件、做表格、写周报、做 PPT、查资料，做完把文件交给你。

### [Praxis Console](https://console.deepraxis.com)

Build and run agents for your business:

- **Service agents** answer your customers on your website, app and business systems.
- **General agents** work with your team on their own cloud computers.
- **Models, sandboxes, knowledge and search** are available to your agents.

在 Console 里搭建和运行你的智能体：

- **服务智能体**：在网站、App 和业务系统里服务你的客户。
- **通用智能体**：和团队一起干活，每个都有自己的云端电脑。
- **模型、沙箱、知识库、搜索**：都是你的智能体可以直接用的能力。

## For developers · 开发者

Everything in the Console is also an API. See the [developer docs](https://docs.deepraxis.com/docs/developers/agent/quickstart) for agents, applications, models, sandboxes, knowledge, search, media and databases.

Console 里的一切都有对应的 API。智能体、应用、模型、沙箱、知识库、搜索、媒体和数据库的用法见[开发者文档](https://docs.deepraxis.com/docs/developers/agent/quickstart)。

```bash
npm install -g @deepraxis/cli
praxis login
```
